I'm a research scientist at Sakana AI working on multi-agent orchestration and reinforcement learning.

# Research Overview

My research focuses on a few themes, these days these are:

- How can we train multi-agent systems to collaborate to access capabilities beyond any individual member
- How can we ensure multi-agent systems are safe, and how do single-agent alignment principles transfer to collectives
- Can agents or collectives perform their own prompt and context optimization to recursively self-improve

I'm also a core member of the [Fugu team](https://sakana.ai/fugu/), where I primarily look after Fugu Ultra and Fugu Cyber.
